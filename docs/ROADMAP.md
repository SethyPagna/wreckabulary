## Scope

**Must have:** Versus "Dibs!" in the living room, tutorial, simple hub with drop-in join and typewriter mode select. *(Prototype of all three in place; needs playtesting, art and audio.)*

**Should have:** Co-op "Moving Day" (1–2 levels), bedroom map.

- Creative "Home Sweet Home" (build, play, save and load rooms)
- More Moving Day levels
- Kitchen and garden maps
- Furnish First variation
- Character and house customisation in the hub
- The letter cat NPC
- Full active ragdoll (fallback: animated movement with ragdoll on hit)
